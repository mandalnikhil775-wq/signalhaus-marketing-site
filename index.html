What is Signalhaus? +

Signalhaus is a fictional analytics platform concept that brings business metrics together and helps teams spot useful patterns. This site is a portfolio demo.

Which tools can I connect? +

The product concept is designed for common analytics, commerce, and customer tools. The dashboard shown here uses sample data and does not connect to live services.

Can I try it before I pay? +

The Starter plan is free in this concept. Use the interactive preview and billing toggle to explore the experience.

Does the dashboard show real company data? +

No. All names, metrics, testimonials, and pricing are illustrative demo content created for this portfolio project.
